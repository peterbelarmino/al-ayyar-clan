About the Al-Ayyar Family

Welcome to the digital home of the Al-Ayyar Family. We are proud to share our heritage, memories, and stories with family members across Kuwait and around the world.

This website serves as our digital archive — a place where photos, videos, and stories from every generation are preserved. From our roots in Al `Abbasiyah, Farwaniya Governorate to wherever life has taken us, we remain connected.
